# M5: RL against BC against the scripted driver against the human

Generated by `python -m python.m5.compare`. Every input is committed under
`results/comparison/`, so this reproduces from a clean clone.

**Read the artefact columns before the statistics.** Three distortions were measured
before this table was built, and each makes an obvious reading false. The steering level
comparison is dominated by track geometry: the generated loop always turns and runs one
way. The smoothness comparison against the human is dominated by the human's input
device: 67.8 per cent of its nonzero steering changes land exactly on the 0.05 lattice,
with modes at 0.15 and 0.20. Neither is a statement about driving skill.
